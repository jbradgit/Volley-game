Recolour Preview

Each source pixel inside a traced layer is tinted with the team's colour for that region while preserving the source pixel's relative brightness. The maths per pixel is output = team_colour × max(R,G,B) / 255. Bright source pixels (e.g. the shirt body) get the full team colour; dark source pixels (the black line-work) get a dark version of the team colour. That's how edge definition survives without a separate "outline" region — the dark pixels are part of whichever layer claims them, they just stay dark within their colour family.
