Workflow

  1. Open a sprite (it's only a tracing backdrop — not used in the game).
  2. Select a layer (Shirt by default). Press B/T and draw its outline as a closed loop.
  3. Repeat for every region. Each region is one layer with its own closed loop.
  4. Hit ▦ Region map — every loop is auto-filled and your outlines stamp black. No manual Fill needed.
  5. ▶ Preview in game and cycle teams to check the fills.
  6. Ctrl+S saves the project (just the outlines — fills are derived).

The only rule: each region's outline must be a closed loop (no gaps) so its inside can be filled. Thickness/precision don't matter — the line itself becomes the black outline. A gap = the fill leaks out (you'll see it instantly on the Region map). F (Fill) is still there for the old shaded pipeline.

How layers work

Each layer is one region (Shirt, Sleeves, …). Click a layer to make it the active one — that's where your strokes go. Click 👁 to hide a layer, 🔒 to lock it, the colour box to recolour it, or the name to rename. Overlapping pixels resolve top-down (top layer wins).
